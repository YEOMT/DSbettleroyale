# Network and prediction policy

- The authoritative simulation advances fixed 1/60-second steps. Incoming messages never add extra steps. Latest movement replaces older pending movement; actions use a separate bounded queue.
- Clients sample at 60 Hz and send two samples per 30 Hz batch. Server acknowledges **applied**, not received, sequence/clientTick, the replacement boundary, held ticks and neutral state. The client removes applied/replaced history and replays at most 12 remaining steps, bounded at 120 historical inputs. Samples older than the acknowledged clientTick plus held ticks are discarded. There is no gameplay replay, damage or sound generation in movement prediction.
- Ordinary movement, wall trace and rectangular boundary geometry share the extracted movement kernel. The 840-tick straight/turn/wall/stop test matches the server within 1e-8 units; another 600 idle ticks show no movement. Server-only impulses, captures, leaps, body collisions and complex control transitions need broader parity. Do not infer their correctness from the ordinary movement test.
- Life, warp and match resets clear history and render offset. Corrections affect simulation immediately; offsets below 50 world units decay only in presentation. No uncertain remote-body force is applied locally.
- Movement becomes neutral after 250 ms without input. Ongoing damage/status effects continue. Expected late life/match packets are rejected without accumulating the malformed-message disconnect counter.
- Remote presentation currently uses 75 ms behind the latest source tick anchored to arrival, with bounded progress and hold after a gap. Clock offset and RTT are measured separately; offset is not yet the sole driver of remote render time. Full interpolation/extrapolation/path fidelity is not claimed.
- Reliable short-shot spawn/end and confirmed FX are bounded at 2,048 records / 1 MiB / 10 seconds per recipient. Resumption uses FULL_STATE and current cursor; it does not replay stale attacks. Native/RW short shots can leave a confirmed endpoint trace. Local previews are cosmetic action markers, not a complete multi-projectile trajectory preview implementation.
- Soft bufferedAmount 64 KiB, hard 256 KiB for 1 second. Snapshots are replaceable; important commands/events are separately bounded. Native ping/pong detects dead sockets. App PING reports RTT; input-applied delay, arrival gaps and correction distance are separate measurements.
- Client retry is exponential with jitter, capped at 8 seconds between attempts and 60 seconds overall. Room expiration, supersession, kick and version mismatch terminate retry with an explanation. Room lifetime defaults to five minutes with no connected members.

`tools/network/run.mjs` injects FIFO application-message delay over actual TCP/WS, not OS packet loss. It records nine RTT/jitter profiles, stalls and one received trace for presentation-delay comparisons. No school Wi-Fi, 26 physical render devices or input-to-photon measurement was performed.
